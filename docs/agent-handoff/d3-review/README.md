Phase 2B decision **D3**: a human reads every rendered surface and adjudicates the claims on it.
This is the designated completeness control. A pattern harness cannot substitute for it — the measured
reach of the automated scan is **6 of 15** ordinary phrasings, and round 21 found nine live false claims
that twenty harness rounds had missed.

**400 distinct claims across 64 rendered surfaces, in 34 batches of 12.**

Every quote is taken from the **rendered page** — what a visitor sees. You are not asked to read any
code, or to find the evidence yourself. A claim appearing on several pages is listed **once**, with
the pages named.

For each claim mark one:

- **KEEP** — true and supportable as written
- **CHANGE** — true in substance but overstated or misleading (say what it should say)
- **REMOVE** — not supportable; withdraw it
- **NEEDS EVIDENCE** — could be true; name what would settle it

Being listed here is **not** an accusation. Recall was favoured over precision: a wrongly-surfaced
claim costs one KEEP; a missed one is the failure this control exists to catch.

## How this is ordered

**Tier 1 — batches 01 to 08 (96 claims).** Safety and approval assertions, review practices,
named reviewers, cadences, universal coverage claims and independence claims. These are the ones where
a wrong claim changes what a school decides. **Start here.**

**Tier 2 — batches 09 to 34 (304 claims).** Descriptive copy about the score system, KCSIE
references, and the containment's own holding statements. Still worth reading — the holding statements
are the containment speaking and their wording matters — but a wrong one here misleads far less.
