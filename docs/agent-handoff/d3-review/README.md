# D3 — human rendered-surface review pack

Phase 2B decision **D3**: a human reads every rendered surface and adjudicates the claims on it.
This is the designated completeness control. A pattern harness cannot substitute for it: in round 21,
a reviewer who simply read the site found **nine live false claims** in the shipped bundle that twenty
rounds of harness work had missed.

The scan's last measured reach was 6 of 15 ordinary phrasings, taken on 29 September 2026 on the
**previous, larger** corpus. It is deliberately **not** quoted as current: the product changed on
30 September and reach has not been re-measured on these bytes.

## Second issue — what changed since the first pack

On 30 September 2026 the Founder reset product scope: AI Equipment and the Prompts library are removed,
and all affiliate links go with them. This pack is regenerated from the **retained** surfaces only.

- Claims whose only surface was Equipment, Prompts or affiliate copy are **not** here. They are
  recorded as **removed by scope** — resolved by deletion, not adjudicated, and not sent back to you.
- Your adjudications on batches 01-04 of the first pack **still stand** wherever the surface stands.
- The **Training** decisions you marked NEEDS EVIDENCE (CPD wording, "Accredited") remain **live and
  open**: training is on hold, not removed, so those claims are still on the site and still need
  settling. They reappear in this pack.
- Affiliate disclosure copy is gone from the site, so the independence claims here are about
  editorial independence only. Note that no tool link ever carried a commission parameter: the
  affiliate-link disclosure on tool pages was unreachable code.

**119 distinct claims across 41 rendered surfaces, in 10 batches of 12.**

Every quote is taken from the **rendered page** — what a visitor sees. You are not asked to read any
code, or to find the evidence yourself. A claim appearing on several pages is listed **once**, with
the pages named.

For each claim mark one:

- **KEEP** — true and supportable as written
- **CHANGE** — true in substance but overstated or misleading (say what it should say)
- **REMOVE** — not supportable; withdraw it
- **NEEDS EVIDENCE** — could be true; name what would settle it

Being listed here is **not** an accusation. Recall was favoured over precision: a wrongly-surfaced
claim costs one KEEP; a missed one is the failure this control exists to catch.

## How this is ordered

**Tier 1 — batches 01 to 05 (60 claims).** Safety and approval assertions, review practices,
named reviewers, cadences, universal coverage claims and independence claims. These are the ones where
a wrong claim changes what a school decides. **Start here.**

**Tier 2 — batches 06 to 10 (59 claims).** Descriptive copy about the score system, KCSIE
references, and the containment's own holding statements. Still worth reading — the holding statements
are the containment speaking and their wording matters — but a wrong one here misleads far less.
