## 3. Why this is the same class as GOV-GAP-001 and GOV-GAP-002

All three are a governed input that an instrument in force depends on and that no available store
holds: the consolidated Methodology v2.2 Standard text (001), Validity System MVB v0.1 (002), and
the Operating System text this containment quotes (003). The irony is on the record: the rule being
cited unreadably is the rule against inventing what is missing.

## 4. Why it does not block the containment

UOS **v2.1 §0.4**, **§24.8** and **Schedule A** address this directly, and they are in the pack:

> "Any provision not listed above — **Unreconciled.** To be carried forward or expressly retired at
> ratification (§0.4, §24.8)."

> "Nothing has been silently dropped; anything absent is, by definition, **unreconciled rather than
> repealed**."

So the three provisions are **unreconciled, not repealed**, and the containment continues to operate
under them. §24.8 records that the reconciliation sweep "is not complete", and that ratification of
v2.1 "does not repeal" an unlisted v1.3.1 provision. Schedule A also confirms that the
**public-language** and **legacy-state** safeguards of v1.3.1 were "merged into §20, §22.4, §10.4,
§9.2" — so the containment's public-language rules have a live destination in the instrument that
*is* readable, even where the originating section is not.

**One citation was verified against source for the first time.** `Rule4bGuard` traces to **UOS v1.2
§6.1 Rule 4b**, which the pack does contain: *"No score artefact leaves the platform without a dated
stamp and a live-score link. Badges must be served live from the GetPromptly endpoint only — static
badge images are never issued."* That citation is sound.
